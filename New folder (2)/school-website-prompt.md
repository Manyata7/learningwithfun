# Website Design Prompt: Kids' School Learning Portal

## Overview
Design and build a colorful, friendly, animated learning website for a school, aimed at children. The site should feel warm, safe, and exciting to explore — like a digital classroom crossed with a storybook. Content is organized by **class (grade level)** and then by **subject**, with an additional section for short motivational and moral stories.

## Visual Style & Colors
- Combine **vibrant, playful colors** (sunny yellow, sky blue, coral pink, grass green) with **soft pastels** (mint, lavender, peach, baby blue) for backgrounds and cards, so it feels energetic but not overwhelming.
- Rounded corners everywhere — buttons, cards, images — nothing sharp or "corporate."
- Hand-drawn or paper-cutout style illustrations rather than stiff stock photos.
- Playful, rounded typography for headings (something like a friendly rounded sans-serif); clean and highly readable body text for actual study content.
- Generous white/soft space so it doesn't feel cluttered despite the color.

## Navigation Structure
- Homepage with a big, welcoming banner and a "Choose Your Class" section — large tappable cards/icons for each grade (e.g., Class 1, Class 2, Class 3...).
- Selecting a class opens a **subject grid** for that class (Math, Science, English, Social Studies, Art, etc.), each subject with its own icon and color theme.
- Each subject page leads to organized lessons/topics, with progress-style visuals (stars, badges, streaks) to make learning feel like an achievement.
- A separate, always-accessible section in the main nav called something like **"Little Stories, Big Lessons"** or **"Be Kind Corner"** — short, powerful, age-appropriate stories that teach honesty, kindness, courage, sharing, etc., each ending with a simple takeaway message.

## Characters & Personality
- Include 2–3 recurring **cute mascot characters** (e.g., a friendly owl teacher, a cheerful bear helper, a curious little robot) who guide kids through the site — popping up with tips, encouragement, or congratulations.
- A **soft, smiling teacher character** (illustrated, not photographic) who welcomes kids on the homepage and appears near story/lesson sections, giving it a nurturing, approachable feel rather than a strict classroom vibe.

## Micro-Animations & Interactivity
- Small delightful animations on interaction: buttons that gently bounce or wiggle on hover/tap, stars that sparkle when a lesson is completed, mascots that wave or blink occasionally.
- Subtle background elements (floating clouds, twinkling stars, drifting leaves) that move slowly to keep the page feeling alive without being distracting.
- Confetti or a cheer animation when a child finishes a lesson or story.
- Smooth, bouncy page transitions (not abrupt jumps) between class → subject → lesson.

## Motivational Stories Section
- Short stories (2–5 minutes read time), told simply with illustrations, each centered on a value: honesty, kindness, hard work, courage, sharing, gratitude, etc.
- End each story with a one-line takeaway or "moral" highlighted in a colorful callout box.
- Optionally, a simple "How did this story make you feel?" reaction section (emoji-based) to keep it interactive.

## Tone & Safety
- Everything kid-friendly, warm, and encouraging — no harsh colors, no scary imagery, no clutter.
- Simple language throughout, large tap targets, and clear icons so young children can navigate mostly on their own.
- Positive reinforcement language everywhere ("Great job!", "You did it!", "Keep going, superstar!").

## English Subject Content (by Class)
- **Class 1 – English:** ABCs (alphabet learning) — include a fun, animated alphabet section with letter sounds, pictures, and mascot-led guidance.
- **Class 3 – English:** Sentence Formation — simple, interactive exercises to help kids build basic sentences.
- **Class 4 – English:** Sentence Formation — slightly more advanced sentence-building exercises.
- **Class 5 – English:** Sentence Formation — further building on sentence structure with more complex examples.

## Maths Subject Content (by Class)
- **Class 1 – Maths:** Numbers 1–50 (fun, visual number recognition with animated counting).
- **Class 2 – Maths:** Numbers 1–50 + Addition — simple addition exercises using the same number range.
- **Class 3 – Maths:** Counting 1–100 + Subtraction.
- **Class 4 – Maths:** Multiplication and Division.
- **Class 5 – Maths:** Fractions and Decimals, plus Multiplication and Division.
- **Times Tables:** Include Tables 1–12 for Class 1 through Class 3, and Tables 1–20 for Class 4 and Class 5 — presented as fun, animated, tap-to-reveal tables.

## Hindi Subject Content (by Class)
- **Class 1 – Hindi:** वर्णमाला (Varnmala/alphabet) — animated letter learning similar in style to the English ABC section.
- **Class 2 – Hindi:** दो अक्षर के शब्द (two-letter words).
- **Class 4 – Hindi:** तीन-चार अक्षर के शब्द (three-four letter words) and Sentence Making (वाक्य बनाना).

## EVS (Environmental Studies) Subject Content (by Class)
- **Class 1 – EVS:** Body Parts, Places (names), Types of Animals.
- **Class 2 – EVS:** Relations (family relationships), Parts of a Plant.
- **Class 3 – EVS:** Family and Friends, Food, Transportation.
- **Class 4 – EVS:** Food for Health, Water Resources, Types of Cooking Methods, Types of Houses.
- **Class 5 – EVS:** Water Cycle, Teeth, Body Parts, Places and Landforms, Health and Environment.
- **Format note:** All EVS topics should be presented as **slow, simple explainer videos** with gentle narration and visuals, paced for young children to easily follow and understand.

## Suggested Pages
1. Home (mascot welcome + class selector)
2. Class → Subject grid
3. Subject → Lessons/Topics
4. Stories & Values corner
5. Simple "About/For Parents & Teachers" page (clean, minimal styling, more informational tone)
